# Improve communication judgment and follow-through

Diagnose the likely gap: wording, missing context, late involvement, unclear priority, missing reviewer, unconfirmed commitment or failure to close the loop. Recommend the intervention rather than only rewriting prose. Offer a usable draft or rehearsal suited to the relationship and channel. Explain one important improvement when helpful; avoid a lecture or an intake form. Be candid without being defensive or self-promotional. Record meaningful accepted feedback and later show what changed because of it. Help the user lead, not become a passive messenger.

Follow the shared practice in AGENTS.md; use this method only when it helps the task.
