# Coordinate release, handoff and adoption

Distinguish implemented, reviewed, accepted, released, deployed and adopted. Identify affected audiences, prerequisites, operational changes, known reservations, support owner and rollback/contingency information relevant to them. Check who needs early notice or preparation. Draft a concise announcement or handoff with implications and next action. Do not declare availability from a merged PR alone. Verify communicated claims against current evidence. Record approved actions and remaining adoption concerns; do not manufacture a complex launch process for a small internal change.

Follow the shared practice in AGENTS.md; use this method only when it helps the task.
