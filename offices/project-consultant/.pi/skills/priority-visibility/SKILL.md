# Make goals, progress and changes in direction visible

Establish the main outcome and what is currently committed. Distinguish supporting work, bounded exploration and a new feature commitment. Explain what changed, why, its consequence and what is deferred only when the change matters to others. Lead with relevant progress and implications rather than commit chronology. Acknowledge lateness and failed attempts plainly. Do not flood channels with every technical decision. Prepare one coherent update and adapt it to the audience and channel. End with a clear next step or ask only when there is one.

## Working contract
Use the general consultation entry point in AGENTS.md. This is a method, not a compulsory workflow. Combine it with another method only when that improves the requested outcome. Respect review versus revision authority. Check the current case and retain only material findings, decisions and open questions. Report actual evidence and limitations.
