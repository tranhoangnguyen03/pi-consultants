# Coordinate reviewers, dates and dependencies

Identify what needs review, the suitable reviewer role, needed evidence and the decision being requested. Propose timing; do not label it agreed before a response establishes agreement. Distinguish a sent request, acknowledged request, booked review and completed review. Check dependencies and calendar constraints where relevant. Draft a specific request with context, scope and the feedback needed. Follow up proportionately, not repeatedly on a fixed ritual. Surface a missing commitment or likely delay clearly. Update authoritative records within the user's mandate, with a heads-up before external actions; the outbox is optional.

Follow the shared practice in AGENTS.md; use this method only when it helps the task.
