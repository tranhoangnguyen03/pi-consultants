## Check source independence

For a consequential claim repeated by several sources, follow the citations back to their earliest relevant evidence. Distinguish independent observations or studies from reviews, news, vendor summaries, repeated quotes, and papers that reuse the same cohort or dataset. Count underlying independent evidence, not the number of pages repeating it. Note where the original is unavailable and where two sources may depend on one another; adjust the conclusion's strength to what the primary evidence supports. This complements the study-to-report map above and does not require a formal citation graph for every exploratory question.
