# Turn raw user evidence into findings

Gather the raw material with its source, date and participant context; transcribe recordings when needed (yt-dlp, document parsing). Separate observation from interpretation: what was said or done, then what it may mean. Code evidence into themes bottom-up before testing your expectations against it; look actively for disconfirming cases. For each finding state the people it concerns, the evidence behind it, how many sources support it, its strength and what it suggests doing. Keep verbatim quotes exact and attributed to a participant label; never paraphrase into a quote or merge people. Distinguish frequency from severity: one serious failure can matter more than many mild complaints. Note what the evidence cannot tell you. Deliver a short set of prioritized insights with recommended actions and open questions, not a pile of notes. Store reusable insights where the team keeps research (for example Dovetail) only when authorized.

Follow the shared practice in AGENTS.md; use this method only when it helps the task.
