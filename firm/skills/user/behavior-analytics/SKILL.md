# Read behaviour data to find where users struggle

Start from a question, not a dashboard. Confirm what each event and property actually measures and whether instrumentation is complete before trusting a number. Use the analytics connector the organization has (PostHog, Mixpanel, Amplitude) for funnels, paths, retention and segments, and session recordings or heatmaps (PostHog, Clarity) to see the behaviour behind a drop-off. Compare segments, such as new versus returning, device or plan, before generalizing. Report numbers with their date range, denominators, filters and known gaps; distinguish correlation from cause. Pair the quantitative where with qualitative why from research or recordings. For experiments, define the hypothesis, primary metric, guardrails, sample size and stopping rule before launch, and do not over-read small or early results. Recommend instrumentation fixes when the data cannot answer the question.

## Working contract
Use the general consultation entry point in AGENTS.md. This is a method, not a compulsory workflow. Combine it with another method only when that improves the requested outcome. Respect review versus revision authority. Check the current case and retain only material findings, decisions and open questions. Report actual evidence and limitations.
