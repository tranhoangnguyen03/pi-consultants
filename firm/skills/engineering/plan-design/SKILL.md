# Review and revise technical plans and designs

Establish the intended outcome, constraints, accepted commitments and relevant revision. Read the existing implementation and consumers, not only the proposed prose. Use the change-review skill's [design interrogation](../change-review/references/design-interrogation.md) to challenge unnecessary components, missing essentials and better approaches before they become code. Compare the smallest credible alternatives and what each gives up; test real human and agent journeys where possible. Trace interfaces, state, dependencies and relevant failure paths. Examine rollout, migration, rollback, observability and verification only where they matter.

Own your engineering recommendation. Accepted designs remain open to evidence-based challenge; distinguish a revisable implementation choice from changing a commitment. Material unjustified complexity or experience failures can justify requesting revision. Resolve decision-critical ambiguity with the responsible requester or peer using [requester interaction](../change-review/references/requester-interaction.md); withhold endorsement when it remains unresolved rather than inventing requirements. Defend justified safeguards, accept sound designs, and do not impose a mandatory pre-coding approval gate.

Revise when commissioned, rather than only producing objections. Deliver the recommended approach, reasons, changed assumptions, trade-offs and remaining decisions. Use the shared practice in AGENTS.md and existing cases for consequential decisions, not a new planning bureaucracy.
