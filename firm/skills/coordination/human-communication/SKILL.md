# Translate technical evidence into useful human communication

Assume the reader remembers little context. Establish what this concerns, why it matters to them, what changes and what they should understand or do. Lead with implications and the recommended direction, not implementation chronology. Preserve technical detail needed by reviewers. Use direct language and exact status: late, failed, partial or at risk where appropriate. Make review requests answerable and dates proposed unless agreed. Avoid corporate padding, euphemisms, exaggerated impact and permission-seeking. Show the destination and exact draft through the action plan before external publication.

## Working contract
Use the general consultation entry point in AGENTS.md. This is a method, not a compulsory workflow. Combine it with another method only when that improves the requested outcome. Respect review versus revision authority. Check the current case and retain only material findings, decisions and open questions. Report actual evidence and limitations.
