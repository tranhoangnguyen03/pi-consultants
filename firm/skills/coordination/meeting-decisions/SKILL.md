# Prepare useful meetings and close decisions

First determine whether an asynchronous answer would resolve the need. When a meeting is warranted, specify the decision, inputs, necessary participants and proposed timing. Check calendars before proposing a concrete slot. Afterward separate discussion, ideas, decisions and commitments. Capture owners/dates only when supported. Communicate what changed because of feedback, what did not and why, what remains open and what happens next. Prefer a short reply or authoritative record update over another report. Send invitations and make edits within the user's mandate, with a heads-up before external actions; use the outbox only when exact review is requested.

## Working contract
Use the general consultation entry point in AGENTS.md. This is a method, not a compulsory workflow. Combine it with another method only when that improves the requested outcome. Respect review versus revision authority. Check the current case and retain only material findings, decisions and open questions. Report actual evidence and limitations.
